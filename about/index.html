About GameBench

Our Mission

GameBench exists to help gamers optimize their setup with free, easy-to-use tools. We believe every player deserves access to clear, accurate performance data — no sign-ups, no subscriptions, no hidden costs. Whether you're building a new PC, tweaking your mouse settings, or troubleshooting lag, our tools give you the numbers you need instantly.

What We Offer

GameBench provides 10 free gaming tools and calculators:

  • Bottleneck Calculator — Check if your CPU or GPU is limiting gaming performance
  • FPS Calculator — Estimate frame rates for your favorite games at different resolutions
  • eDPI Calculator — Calculate your effective mouse sensitivity for precise aiming
  • Sensitivity Converter — Convert mouse sensitivity between CS2, Valorant, Apex, and more
  • DPI Analyzer — Test your mouse's true DPI and verify sensor accuracy
  • PC Build Cost Calculator — Plan your gaming PC budget and compare component costs
  • Ping & Latency Calculator — Estimate network delay and optimize your connection
  • Refresh Rate vs FPS Calculator — Understand the relationship between refresh rate and frame rate
  • FOV Calculator — Calculate optimal field of view for your monitor and seating distance
  • Monitor Distance Calculator — Find the ideal viewing distance for your monitor size

Our Principles

  • Free Forever — All tools are completely free to use. No premium tiers, no paywalls, no email required.
  • Privacy First — Every calculation runs directly in your browser. Your data never leaves your device. We collect zero personal information.
  • Accuracy — Our tools use real hardware benchmarks and established formulas to provide reliable estimates.
  • No Registration — Use any tool instantly. No account creation, no email verification, no login required.

Important Disclaimer

GameBench is NOT a professional hardware consulting service. Results are estimates based on general benchmarks and data. They are not guarantees of actual performance and should not replace professional hardware reviews or advice. Always consult professional reviews and benchmarks before making hardware purchasing decisions.
